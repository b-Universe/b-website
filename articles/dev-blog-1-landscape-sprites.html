Dev Blog #1: Landscape & Sprites

Setting the Stage

Welcome to the very first development blog for B! When we first conceptualized this project, we knew that the environment itself needed to feel both expansive and intimately detailed. Our very first steps into building the world began by tackling the general landscape and our sprite usage.

General Landscape

Generating the landscape was our primary hurdle. We wanted to move away from standard generation algorithms and create something that felt tailor-made for our vision. Using a combination of custom JavaScript logic and meticulous canvas tweaking, we established the foundation of an immersive world.

Sprite Usage & Immersion

Once the ground was laid, the world needed life and character. That's where our custom sprites came into play. By utilizing custom JavaScript rendering and clever CSS styling, we were able to introduce a classic top-down 3D visual in a 2D world, environmental details like basic blocks, and custom UI elements that seamlessly blend into our web environment.

Bringing the Player to Life

For our initial character animations, we owe heavy credit to Xzany's Top-Down Adventurer Character. They presented us with a wonderful 4-directional player sprite with several fluid animations that served as the perfect starting point for our development.

As a starting basis, we also utilized various pixel-art textures kindly provided without restrictive licenses to build out the early environment.

Getting the first custom sprite to render correctly against our new landscape was an incredibly automagical moment. It really brought the whole concept to life!

Looking Ahead:

8-Directional Movement

As we continue to grow and refine the B experience, our research is already extending into 8-directional development! We'll be utilizing fantastic resources - like this 8-directional top-down pixel art tutorial - to help us concept and build a fully custom, automagical movement system that feels wonderfully fluid!

A quick preview of our next sprite models we're creating! They're going to take a lot of time, but I have that given my current living situation.
