Minecraft Development

I've spent an embarrassing amount of my life working with Minecraft servers.

If you're running a server, would like one set up for you/established, or need custom mechanics, scripting, automation, backend systems, resource packs, datapacks, NPCs, or server management help, there's a Great chance I can help.

I've worked extensively with Paper, BungeeCord, Citizens, Denizen, and various custom server setups over the years. Some people build houses in Minecraft; I apparently decided to build infrastructure instead.

A common misunderstanding is that while Minecraft supports custom servers and add-ons, people often assume the experience will feel low-quality or limited. In reality, Minecraft is one of the most flexible platforms available and can be an incredible extension of an existing gaming community.

I've personally built large-scale projects from scratch, including a recreation of Old School RuneScape's Castle Wars minigame entirely within Minecraft!

Beyond custom content, a Minecraft server can provide something many communities struggle with: long-term retention. Players naturally cycle between games, wait for updates, or simply want a change of pace. Having a community Minecraft server gives those players a familiar place to return to while still staying connected with your community, your Discord, and your events. Instead of disappearing entirely, they remain engaged with the people they've met along the way.

In my experience, a well-run Minecraft server isn't just another game server; it's often a social hub that keeps a community active between major releases, content updates, and gaming trends.

The Process

I like keeping things simple; First we talk about the project. Then we figure out exactly what you need. After that we'll agree on pricing, timelines, expectations, and what the finished result should look like.

I strongly prefer discussing project details here in our post, or documented conversation whenever possible. It protects both of us and makes sure nobody has to rely on memory later. For paid service-based work, I typically require 50% upfront and 50% upon completion.

If you're interested in working together, feel free to reach out! Even if I end up not being the right person for the job, I'm happy to point people in the right direction. I'll provide support to help you find what you need!
