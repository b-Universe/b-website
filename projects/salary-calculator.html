Salary Calculator

About This Calculator

The Salary Calculator converts salary amounts to their corresponding values based on payment frequency. Examples of payment frequencies include biweekly, semi-monthly, or monthly payments. Results include unadjusted figures and adjusted figures that account for vacation days and holidays per year.

Calculation Methodology

Using a $30 hourly rate, an average of eight hours worked each day, and 260 working days a year (52 weeks multiplied by 5 working days a week), the annual unadjusted salary can be calculated as: $30 x 8 x 260 = $62,400.

The adjusted annual salary can be calculated as: $30 x 8 x (260 - 25) = $56,400. Using 10 holidays and 15 paid vacation days a year, subtract these non-working days from the total number of working days a year. All bi-weekly, semi-monthly, monthly, and quarterly figures are derived from these annual calculations.

Different Pay Frequencies

  • Daily: Pays every day, usually at the end of the day.
  • Weekly: Pays once each week, usually on Fridays. Relatively costly for employers with 52 weeks a year, resulting in higher payroll processing costs.
  • Bi-Weekly: Pays every two weeks, which comes out to 26 times a year for most years.
  • Semi-Monthly: Pays twice a month, usually on the fifteenth and final day of the month, resulting in 24 paychecks a year.
  • Monthly: Pays on a specified day once a month.
  • Quarterly: Pays 4 times a year.
  • Semi-Annually: Pays 2 times a year.
  • Annually: Pays once a year.

Factors that Influence Salary in the U.S.

Most statistics below are from the U.S. Bureau of Labor in 2025. In the third quarter of 2025, the average salary of a full-time employee in the U.S. is $1,214 per week, which equates to $63,128 per year.

  • Age: Men aged 35 to 44 had the highest annual earnings at $78,208, and women earned the most between the ages of 35 and 44 at $63,752.
  • Education: Workers 25 or over without a high school degree had median earnings of $40,404 compared to $50,960 for high school graduates. Workers with at least bachelor's degrees earned $90,844 annually on average.
  • Experience: The further entrenched a person is in their career, the more experience they have, or the more valuable their skillset, the higher their salary tends to be.
  • Race and Ethnicity: Black men earned a median salary of $53,664, compared to white men at $70,824. Black women earned $48,204 compared to white women at $57,356. Hispanic and Asian people of both genders earned $49,088 and $84,240, respectively.
  • Gender: Men earned an average salary of $69,316, and women earned $55,952.
  • Industry: Industry affects wages paid, even in similar roles.
  • Location: Different locations have different supplies and demands for positions, and average salaries reflect this.

Federal laws do not mandate pay frequency, except that employees must be paid in routine and predictable manners. The federal minimum wage rate overrides lower state minimums; for example, Georgia's minimum wage is set at $5.15, but the $7.25 federal minimum rate overrides it.
