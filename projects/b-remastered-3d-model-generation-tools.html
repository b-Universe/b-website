B Remastered 3D Model Generation Tools

A suite of six standalone browser-based procedural 3D asset studios built for B Remastered. Every studio pairs deterministic seed-driven geometry and stylized low-poly materials with interactive Three.js viewports, versioned JSON blueprints, and one-click binary .GLB exports ready for game worlds.

Studio 01 · Urban Architecture b.building.v1

3D Building Generator

Construct multi-story urban blocks, apartments, and storefronts one brick at a time across 8 architectural styles including Red Brick, Brownstone, Downtown, Art Deco, Modern, and Derelict.

  • Customizable footprints, window bays, fire escapes, stoops, and awnings
  • Rooftop water tanks, HVAC units, chimneys, antennas, and interior cutaways
  • Weathering, age, damage, and graffiti controls with .GLB & blueprint export
Open 3D Building Generator
Studio 02 · High-Rise Skylines b.skyscraper.v1

3D Skyscraper Generator

Sculpt towering city landmarks and multi-tower complexes across 22 high-rise styles, from Art Deco spires and Neo-Gothic monoliths to curved ribbon glass towers and skybridge-linked twins.

  • Stacked podium, lower, middle, upper, crown, and rooftop spire sections
  • Twist, taper, setback, slanted-top, and exoskeleton structural sculpting
  • Silhouette inspection mode, deterministic seeds, and .GLB & blueprint export
Open 3D Skyscraper Generator
Studio 05 · Purpose-Built Lots b.specialty-building.v1

3D Specialty Building Suite

Generate complete purpose-built commercial, civic, healthcare, automotive, hospitality, and industrial properties spanning 10 categories such as gas stations, restaurants, hospitals, banks, and police precincts.

  • Three-layer architecture separating Building, Property Lot, and City Object props
  • Configurable parking stalls, drive-thru and fuel lanes, signage, and roof types
  • Era presets, condition weathering, 1.82m scale reference, and layer-scoped .GLB export
Open 3D Specialty Building Suite
Botanical Studio · Flora & Groves b.tree.v1

3D Flora & Tree Studio

Craft 20 botanical species across 7 ecosystems-from broadleaf oaks, maples, and sakura to baobabs, redwoods, palms, shrubs, bonsai, and fantasy trees-built for natural parks and city streetscapes.

  • Real-time wind physics, biome grove layouts, and deterministic variation locks
  • 4-stage LOD meshes (LOD0–LOD3) plus foliage billboard atlas generation
  • Visual 3D flora catalog browser with one-click .GLB and .tree.json export
Open 3D Flora & Tree Studio
Vehicle Studio · Urban Fleet b.vehicle.v1

3D City Vehicles Studio

Design stylized low-poly vehicles across 23 archetypes covering compact cars, sedans, coupes, SUVs, taxis, pickups, cargo vans, box trucks, city & articulated buses, semi trucks, and emergency fleets.

  • Proportional chassis, wheelbase, cab-to-cargo split, roofline, and glass controls
  • Live headlight illumination, flashing emergency lightbars, and 7 fleet paint palettes
  • Lock-aware preset randomization with .GLB and .b.vehicle.json blueprint export
Open 3D City Vehicles Studio
Studio 04 · Props & Interiors b.city-object.v1

3D City Object Suite

Browse and customize a massive procedural catalog of 115 single object families and 805 presets across 30 categories-plus 54 multi-object vignette assemblies-spanning sidewalks, rooftops, offices, clinics, arcades, households, and seasonal holiday props.

  • Faceted search by space, environment tag, attachment profile, style, and condition
  • Interactive screen & sign surfaces, placement anchors, and B-Units dimensions
  • Thumbnail catalog modal, JSON blueprint import/export, and binary .GLB output
Open 3D City Object Suite
All six studios run 100% client-side in your browser with Three.js-no external backend required. Share seeds or exported .json blueprints between sessions to reproduce exact models, or drop exported .GLB meshes straight into your B Remastered scenes.
