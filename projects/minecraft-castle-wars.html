Minecraft Castle Wars

Released: May 1, 2019

Welcome to Castle Wars!

Today, we are absolutely thrilled to announce the release of our completely custom recreation of the iconic Castle Wars minigame from Old School RuneScape (OSRS)! This project has been an absolute blast to build, and we have meticulously translated the classic Capture the Flag experience directly into our Minecraft server using Denizen.

Playing this feels exactly like the original. We spent weeks ensuring the layout is completely accurate to the OSRS map and tuning the scripts so that every mechanic operates smoothly and faithfully. Grab your team's cape, rush the enemy castle, and defend your flag at all costs!

Mechanics & Gameplay

To capture the true essence of Castle Wars, we implemented the core interactive features you know and love. Just like OSRS, you can set up barricades to block crucial choke points. Players can attack these barricades manually to break them down or use explosive potions for instant destruction.

Watch your step with those potions, though! Explosive potions deal massive damage to barricades but will severely hurt you if you are caught in the blast radius. Furthermore, the underground caves aren't just for sneaking around the map. You can use your pickaxe or those same explosive potions to collapse the tunnels, blocking off shortcuts and crushing anyone unlucky enough to be standing underneath!

Visual Effects & Target Tracking

To ensure fair play and intense chases, we built a highly robust flag tracking system. To make sure no one can simply hide with the flag underground or try to glitch out of bounds, we designed a dual-layer particle tracking system. A vibrant, colored particle trail constantly follows the flag bearer. If they try to go deep into the caves, an overhead beacon still signals their location to players on the surface!

We also put a lot of effort into the respawn safe rooms. When the game starts, the energy barriers activate. We created a seamless portal transition to drop you right into your team's spawn area with excellent visual feedback.

Objectives & Interface

The ultimate goal is, of course, taking the enemy's standard! Stealing the enemy flag and bringing it all the way back to your base triggers fantastic custom firework animations and broadcasts to the whole map.

We also built a custom, completely automated UI system for the lobby. Everything from your team's score to the waiting room countdown is neatly displayed using boss bars and scoreboard elements, ensuring you always know exactly how much time is left.

Lobby & Auto-Balancing

We wanted the matchmaking to be as seamless as possible. Waiting in the lobby is fully automated, but we went a step further for game health. To ensure fair matches, the green portal (Guthix) automatically sorts players into the team with the fewest active members.
