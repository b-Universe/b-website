skin command

denizen script

wardrobe control

changing and managing character skins in minecraft typically requires quitting to the launcher or web browser. this full-featured script gives players complete in-game control over their wardrobe.

players can save their favorite outfits, copy skins from any online player by username, import custom textures from direct image urls (with slim or regular model selection), and switch looks on the fly.

looking for new skins? websites like minecraftskins.com have thousands of community creations. when you find a skin you like, use the Image Link button on their site to get the direct URL. then, just use /skin <url> in-game to apply it instantly!

commands & syntax

the /skin command dispatcher is built for ease of use. applying a skin immediately syncs the new texture model across the network to all surrounding players. here are the available commands:

/skin <url / player / default>

instantly apply a skin by providing a direct image URL, targeting another online player's username, or using default to clear your current skin.

/skin save <name>

saves your currently applied skin texture under a custom name, allowing you to easily switch back to it later.

/skin set <name>

equips a previously saved skin from your wardrobe. features smart tab-completion for all your saved outfits.

/skin list

displays a clickable, formatted chat menu of all the skins you currently have saved in your wardrobe.

/skin delete <name>

permanently removes a saved skin from your wardrobe organization flags.

/skin rename <old_name> <new_name>

updates the name of a previously saved outfit without needing to re-apply and re-save the texture.
