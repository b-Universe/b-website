Privacy Policy

Last updated September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how B Remastered, the B Launcher, and related B services (collectively, “B”) handle information when you play, create an account, or link Discord.

Information We Collect

We collect the information needed to operate B and keep the service safe:

  • Account information: username, optional email address, and a password stored only as a salted, memory-hard derived-key hash.
  • Game information: characters, progression, settings, social relationships, alliance and party activity, moderation history, and other information created through play.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser or launcher details, connection diagnostics, and security or error logs.
  • Discord information: only if you choose to link Discord, we store the Discord user ID, username or display name, avatar reference, and the time of linking.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide accounts and gameplay, save your progress, support social features, prevent abuse, investigate technical problems, enforce our rules, and communicate service or security notices. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Where Information Is Shared

Information is shared only when necessary to run B. This includes hosting and infrastructure providers, Discord when you choose its linking flow, and authorities where disclosure is required by law or needed to protect players, B, or the public. Other players may see the public in-game information you choose or create, such as character names, alliance membership, and chat messages.

Cookies and Local Storage

B may use browser storage for game sessions, settings, and interface preferences. The B Launcher may use operating-system-protected storage for credentials only when you choose Remember Password. B does not use advertising trackers.

Retention and Security

We keep account and game information while it is needed to operate B, resolve disputes, enforce our rules, or meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, including password hashing, but no online service can promise absolute security.

Your Choices

You may choose not to provide an optional email address, may unlink Discord when that feature is available, and may request help with account information through the B Discord. We may need to verify that a request comes from the account owner before acting on it.

Children and Changes

B is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. We may update this policy as B evolves; the updated date above will change when we do.
