Standard Panel (Col 1)

The default .panel applies a glassmorphic blurred background, border radius, and inset shadow. Best for general wrappers and floating cards.

You can add padding manually via utility classes or inline styles as needed.

Panel Solid (Col 2)

The .panel-solid variant is opaque, removes the box-shadow, and disables the backdrop filter.
